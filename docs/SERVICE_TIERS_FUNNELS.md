# Nexxus Lab - Service Tiers & Conversion Funnels

## 🎯 Service Tier Strategy

### Tier Overview

| Tier | Target Client | Scope | Conversion Goal |
|------|--------------|-------|-----------------|
| **Starter** | SMEs, Startups | Quick wins | Build trust |
| **Growth** | Scaling businesses | Full solutions | Long-term partnership |
| **Enterprise** | Large orgs | Complex needs | Custom solutions |

> **Note:** Pricing is intentionally hidden. All tiers display "Let's Talk" to encourage direct contact and allow custom quoting based on project scope.

---

## 💼 Detailed Service Tiers

### Tier 1: STARTER
**"Get Started Fast"**

**Target Audience:**
- Small businesses needing digital presence
- Startups with MVP requirements
- Businesses automating single processes

**Services Included:**
- Landing page / single-page website
- Mobile responsive design
- Basic chatbot setup
- Simple automation (1-2 workflows)
- Basic SEO setup

**Timeline:** 2-4 weeks  
**Support:** 30 days post-launch

**Conversion Triggers:**
- Low barrier to entry
- Quick results
- Testimonials from similar clients

---

### Tier 2: GROWTH (Most Popular)
**"Scale Your Operations"**

**Target Audience:**
- Growing companies needing efficiency
- Businesses ready for digital transformation
- Teams wanting multiple integrations

**Services Included:**
- Multi-page website with CMS
- Custom AI chatbot with training
- Multiple workflow automations
- Mobile app (cross-platform)
- API integrations
- Analytics dashboard
- UI/UX design process

**Timeline:** 1-3 months  
**Support:** 90 days + maintenance option

**Conversion Triggers:**
- "Most Popular" badge
- Case studies with metrics
- Comparison with DIY alternatives

---

### Tier 3: ENTERPRISE
**"Custom Solutions at Scale"**

**Target Audience:**
- Large organizations
- Complex technical requirements
- Multi-department implementations

**Services Included:**
- Custom software development
- Enterprise AI solutions
- Full system integration
- Dedicated project manager
- SLA guarantees
- On-site training
- Priority support
- Scalability planning

**Timeline:** 3-6+ months  
**Support:** Ongoing partnership

**Conversion Triggers:**
- Discovery call requirement
- Custom proposal process
- References from enterprise clients
- Security/compliance assurances

## 🔄 Conversion Funnels

### Funnel 1: Self-Service (Starter Tier)

```
AWARENESS
    ↓ Google/Social/Referral
INTEREST  
    ↓ Home page → Services page
CONSIDERATION
    ↓ Pricing comparison → Starter tier
INTENT
    ↓ "Get Started" CTA
ACTION
    ↓ Contact form (simple)
CONVERSION
    ✅ Lead for Starter package
```

**Optimization Points:**
- Clear pricing on services page
- Low-commitment CTA language
- Fast response SLA (< 24 hours)

---

### Funnel 2: Consultative (Growth Tier)

```
AWARENESS
    ↓ Content marketing / Referral
INTEREST
    ↓ Workflows page → Case studies
CONSIDERATION
    ↓ Multiple page visits → Newsletter signup
NURTURE
    ↓ Email sequence (3-5 emails)
INTENT
    ↓ "Book a Discovery Call" CTA
QUALIFICATION
    ↓ Discovery call
ACTION
    ↓ Custom proposal
CONVERSION
    ✅ Growth tier contract
```

**Optimization Points:**
- Lead magnet (automation guide PDF)
- Email nurture sequence
- Calendar booking integration
- Proposal template ready

---

### Funnel 3: Enterprise Sales

```
AWARENESS
    ↓ Industry events / Partnerships / Referrals
INTEREST
    ↓ Direct outreach / Inbound inquiry
QUALIFICATION
    ↓ Initial call → Needs assessment
DISCOVERY
    ↓ Technical deep-dive meeting
PROPOSAL
    ↓ Custom solution proposal
NEGOTIATION
    ↓ Stakeholder alignment
CONVERSION
    ✅ Enterprise contract
```

**Optimization Points:**
- Case studies with enterprise clients
- Security/compliance documentation
- Executive summary materials
- Flexible payment terms

## 📈 Pricing Psychology

### Anchoring Strategy
Display Enterprise tier first (or prominently) to make Growth tier feel accessible.

### Decoy Effect
Starter tier serves as anchor; Growth tier positioned as "best value."

### Social Proof Placement
- Starter: "Perfect for getting started"
- Growth: "Most Popular" + client logos
- Enterprise: "Trusted by industry leaders"
